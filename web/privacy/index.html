Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LuckyPeek handles information when you use the LuckyPeek mobile application. LuckyPeek is a casual luck game designed for entertainment.

Birthday input

You may enter a birthday as part of the game experience. The current version uses that birthday only within the on-device game flow. LuckyPeek does not send the birthday to the Global Lucky Feed or Analytics, does not display it publicly, and does not use it to make factual predictions.

Data stored on your device

LuckyPeek may store an anonymous local identifier, Daily Peek state, and app configuration or state on your device. This supports features such as remembering remaining Peeks and resetting them at local midnight. Removing the app may remove this local data, subject to normal iOS behavior.

Global Lucky Feed

When the Global Lucky Feed is available, LuckyPeek sends only limited anonymous game information needed for the Feed, such as a result category, score, timestamp, and server-generated identifier. It does not send your birthday, username, email address, Apple ID, or contacts to the Feed.

Rewarded advertising and consent

LuckyPeek uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for rewarded advertising. Google and its partners may process technical information, coarse location, device identifiers, advertising data, consent choices, app interactions, crash data, performance data, and other diagnostic data for advertising, analytics, app functionality, and diagnostics according to their policies and the choices available to you. Google’s iOS SDK privacy disclosure identifies some Device ID use as tracking under Apple’s definitions. Where applicable, LuckyPeek uses Google User Messaging Platform to request and manage advertising consent, and provides Privacy Options in the app when Google requires that entry point.

LuckyPeek does not currently request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not include an ATT prompt. Your device settings and consent choices may limit advertising identifiers and advertising data available to Google and its partners.

Analytics

LuckyPeek has an analytics adapter for measuring app interactions, but the current production configuration does not use a third-party analytics provider. If a provider is enabled later, this Policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before that change is released. Birthday input is not included in LuckyPeek Analytics events.

Sharing

You can choose to create a Share Card and open the iOS system Share Sheet. You decide which supported activity receives the card. LuckyPeek does not read your contacts and does not automatically send content. Completion of the system share flow does not mean another person received, opened, or acted on the content.

Data retention

Local game state remains on your device until it is reset, cleared, or removed with the app. The Global Lucky Feed keeps at most 50 anonymous entries and expires after 24 hours without Feed activity; it is not a permanent user history. Advertising partners may apply their own retention periods under their published policies.

Children

LuckyPeek is a general-audience entertainment game. It is not designed to collect a child’s name, email address, contacts, or public profile. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information should use the support channel described below.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to enter a birthday, not to share a result, and not to complete a rewarded ad. Local information can generally be removed by clearing the app’s data or deleting the app. For questions, privacy requests, or deletion requests, visit LuckyPeek Support or email luckypeek.support@gmail.com.

Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated when LuckyPeek’s features, service providers, or legal obligations change. The effective date above will identify the current version.

Entertainment disclaimer

LuckyPeek results are randomly generated for entertainment purposes only. They are not factual predictions or medical, financial, legal, or other professional advice.
