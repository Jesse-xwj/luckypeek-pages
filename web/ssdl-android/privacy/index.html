1. About this policy

Space Station Defense Line (SSDL) for Android is operated by JESSE X. This policy covers this Android game and its Android privacy/support website. It is available offline in the app and online in English and Simplified Chinese. Support and privacy contact: Jessie, j39263012@gmail.com.

2. Data on your device

The game stores mission progress, scores and endless records, language, audio and control preferences, tutorial completion, and recent run history locally in its app container. Run history includes a run identifier, dates, configuration snapshots, random seed, results and active playing time. These records support gameplay, records and local history. The game does not send these records to the operator.

3. No automatic collection or tracking

The public game has no account or login, advertising, in-app purchases, analytics SDK, third-party crash-reporting SDK, advertising identifier access, or game server. It does not automatically transmit gameplay, device identifiers or personal information to the operator. It does not implement cloud synchronization. It requests no camera, microphone, contacts or location access. Music and sound effects play bundled audio; they do not record audio.

4. Optional support email

Contact Support opens your email app or lets you copy our address. The game does not automatically attach logs, saved games or device information, and does not send email for you. If you choose to send a message, we receive the sender address, message and anything you voluntarily include. We use this information to respond and resolve the request. Your email provider processes the message under its own terms. Please avoid sending sensitive information or unnecessary personal data. Copy Email writes our support address to your device clipboard only after you tap the button; the game does not read clipboard contents.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information or use support messages for advertising or tracking. Email providers process messages for delivery and support management. Disclosure may be required by applicable law. Android, device manufacturers and app distribution services may process information under their own policies and your device choices, separate from game-operated collection. This game does not add analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.

6. Website visits

The online privacy and support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. Opening the online version is optional and starts only after your action in the system browser. We add no analytics, advertising, remote fonts or tracking scripts to these pages. GitHub states that Pages visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not the visitor is signed in. Visiting the website does not upload your game saves. See GitHub's Privacy Statement at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

7. Retention, deletion and backups

Progress, records and preferences are stored in this Android app’s private files. Recent history is limited to 100 runs. Uninstalling the app or using Android’s Clear storage normally removes these local files; reinstalling does not automatically import iPhone saves. This app disables Android backup in its manifest and excludes game data from Android 12+ cloud backup/device-transfer rules, but system or manufacturer behavior is ultimately controlled by your device. Files deliberately exported or shared elsewhere remain separate copies and must be deleted from those locations. There is no account to delete. Support messages are kept as needed to address requests and applicable obligations; contact the privacy email to request deletion, subject to those obligations.

8. Children and safety

The game does not ask players for age or personal details. The operator does not knowingly request personal information from children through gameplay. If a child needs support, a parent or guardian should contact us. If you believe a child's personal information has been sent to us, contact the privacy email so we can review and address it. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection.

9. Internal builds and changes

Development/internal builds can store tuning presets and test history and use Android’s system file picker to import or export JSON only after the tester acts. No all-files storage permission is requested. Release builds exclude these developer/file-control entry points. If you choose a cloud file provider, that provider handles the selected file under its own policy. Audio uses Android media volume and audio focus, not an iPhone silent switch. Haptics are optional and safely unavailable on unsupported devices. Updates to this policy will carry an updated version and effective date on this website and in a corresponding app update. The game does not fetch policy updates automatically.

10. Local diagnostics and device services

The game uses display dimensions and system audio/lifecycle status locally to arrange the interface and pause playback or gameplay. The engine and Android may produce local diagnostic messages. The game has no automatic log upload or third-party crash reporting. Android, the app store and other device services may independently process diagnostics under their own settings and policies. Optional support messages are separate from these local records.
