LuckyPeek Support

About LuckyPeek

LuckyPeek is a casual entertainment game that generates playful luck scores and funny messages. Its results are random and are not factual predictions or professional advice.

Common issues

Rewarded ad not loading

Check your internet connection, try again later, or close and reopen LuckyPeek. Rewarded ad availability can vary, so an ad may not always be ready.

Peek was not deducted correctly

A Peek should be consumed only after the rewarded flow completes successfully. Cancelling an ad, an unavailable ad, or a presentation failure should not deduct a Peek. Reopen the app to refresh the displayed state if needed.

When do Peeks reset?

You receive 3 Base Peeks per local calendar day. You can earn up to 3 additional Share Bonus Peeks that day. Daily state resets at midnight according to your device’s local time.

How does Share Bonus work?

A completed, supported system share flow can grant 1 Bonus Peek, up to 3 per local calendar day. You may continue sharing after reaching 3/3, but no additional Peek will be granted. A completed share flow does not guarantee that another person received or opened the content.

Lucky Feed is unavailable

The Global Lucky Feed is optional. If it is offline or unavailable, the core LuckyPeek experience should continue to work.
