Contact

Contact JESSE X at xwj020114@gmail.com. Include a description of the issue only if you choose. No logs or device details are attached automatically.

How to play

Move and rotate energy blocks, then complete a row to fire its defense laser. Defend the station gate and keep the top spawn area clear.

Missions and revive

Timed missions require 1, 2, 3 or 4 minutes of active play by lane count. Endless has no time limit. The first failure in a run offers one free revive; there are no ads.

Language and audio

Open Station Settings to choose English or Simplified Chinese and change sound, music or haptics. If audio is silent, check the game's audio settings and the device silent switch.

Data

Progress and preferences are local. There is no account recovery or cloud sync service. See the privacy policy for deletion and backup details.

联系

联系JESSE X：xwj020114@gmail.com。你可自愿描述问题，游戏不会自动附加日志或设备资料。

如何游玩

移动、旋转能量块并补满整行，触发对应路线防御激光。守住左侧舱门，并保持顶部出生位置畅通。

关卡与复活

限时关按路线数要求累计1、2、3或4分钟有效时间，无尽无时限。每局首次失败可免费复活一次，不播放广告。

语言与声音

在空间站设置切换English或简体中文，并调整音效、音乐和触感。无声时请检查游戏声音设置和设备静音开关。

数据

进度和偏好保存在设备本地，没有账号恢复或云同步服务。删除与备份详情见隐私政策。
