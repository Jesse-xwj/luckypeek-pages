About this policy

Space Station Defense Line (SSDL) is operated by JESSE X. This policy describes the public iPhone game's local data, optional support contact, and the privacy and support website. Contact: xwj020114@gmail.com.

Data on your device

The game stores mission progress, scores and endless records, language, audio and control preferences, tutorial completion, and recent run history locally in its app container. Run history includes a run identifier, dates, configuration snapshots, random seed, results and active playing time. These records support gameplay, records and local history. The game does not send these records to the operator.

No automatic collection or tracking

The public game has no account or login, advertising, in-app purchases, analytics SDK, third-party crash-reporting SDK, advertising identifier access, or game server. It does not automatically transmit gameplay, device identifiers or personal information to the operator. It does not implement cloud synchronization. It requests no camera, microphone, contacts or location access. Music and sound effects play bundled audio; they do not record audio.

Optional support email

Contact Support opens your email app or lets you copy our address. The game does not automatically attach logs, saved games or device information, and does not send email for you. If you choose to send a message, we receive the sender address, message and anything you voluntarily include. We use this information to respond and resolve the request. Your email provider processes the message under its own terms. Please avoid sending sensitive information or unnecessary personal data.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information or use support messages for advertising or tracking. Messages may be processed by the email services used to deliver and manage support. We may disclose information if required by applicable law. Apple provides App Store, TestFlight and optional device backup or diagnostic services under Apple's policies and your system choices; this is separate from a game-operated collection service. TestFlight may make feedback and crash information available to the developer.

Website visits

The online privacy and support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. Opening the online version is optional and starts only after your action in the system browser. We add no analytics, advertising, remote fonts or tracking scripts to these pages. GitHub states that Pages visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not the visitor is signed in. Visiting the website does not upload your game saves. See GitHub's Privacy Statement at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

Retention, deletion and backups

Local progress and settings remain until the app data is removed. Recent run history is limited to 100 runs. Deleting the app through iOS normally removes its local app container; offloading the app may keep its documents and data. Copies in device backups are controlled by Apple, your backup settings and your backup provider, and may be restored. Files you previously exported from an internal development build or sent elsewhere are separate copies and must be deleted from their saved locations. There is no game account to delete. Support messages are kept as needed to address the request and meet applicable obligations; no fixed retention period is promised. You can contact us to request deletion of support information, subject to applicable obligations.

Children and safety

The game does not ask players for age or personal details. The operator does not knowingly request personal information from children through gameplay. If a child needs support, a parent or guardian should contact us. If you believe a child's personal information has been sent to us, contact the privacy email so we can review and address it. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection.

Internal builds and changes

Internal development builds can store local tuning presets, detailed test history, and JSON files that testers explicitly import or export. These developer controls are not available in the public Release game. We will update this policy when practices change and display the revised version and effective date in the app and on the website. Future features are not described as current data practices.

简体中文

关于本政策

空间站防线（Space Station Defense Line，简称SSDL）由JESSE X运营。本政策说明公开iPhone游戏的本地数据、自愿支持联系及隐私和支持网站。联系邮箱：xwj020114@gmail.com。

设备上的数据

游戏将关卡进度、分数和无尽纪录、语言、声音和按键偏好、教学完成状态及近期对局历史保存在本App的本地容器。历史包含对局标识、日期、配置快照、随机种子、结果和有效游戏时间，用于游戏功能、纪录与本地历史。游戏不会将这些记录发送给运营者。

无自动收集或跟踪

公开游戏没有账号登录、广告、内购、分析SDK、第三方崩溃上报SDK、广告标识符访问或游戏服务器，不会自动向运营者传输游戏数据、设备标识符或个人信息，也没有实现云同步。游戏不请求相机、麦克风、通讯录或定位权限。音乐和音效使用内置音频，不录制声音。

自愿发送支持邮件

联系支持会打开你的邮件App或让你复制邮箱。游戏不会自动附加日志、存档或设备信息，也不会替你发送邮件。如果你主动发送，我们会收到发件地址、邮件正文及你自愿附加的内容，并用于答复和解决请求。你的邮件服务商按其自身条款处理邮件。请避免发送敏感或不必要的个人信息。

分享与服务提供方

我们不出售个人信息，不将支持邮件用于广告或跟踪。用于收发和管理支持的邮件服务可能处理邮件；适用法律要求时，我们可能披露相关信息。Apple按其政策和你的系统设置提供App Store、TestFlight、可选设备备份及诊断服务，这与游戏运营者自建的数据收集服务不同。TestFlight可能向开发者提供反馈和崩溃信息。

网站访问

在线隐私和支持页面由GitHub Pages托管。只有你主动点击在线版本后才通过系统浏览器访问；游戏启动不会自动请求网站。网页没有添加分析、广告、远程字体或跟踪脚本。GitHub说明，无论访问者是否登录，Pages都会为安全目的记录和保存访问IP。访问网站不会上传游戏存档。GitHub隐私声明：https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement。

保留、删除与备份

本地进度和设置保留至App数据被移除；近期历史最多保存100局。通过iOS删除App通常会删除本地容器；卸载App（保留文稿与数据）可能保留数据。设备备份由Apple、你的备份设置和备份提供方管理，恢复备份可能恢复数据。你曾在内部开发版导出的文件或发送到其他位置的副本独立存在，需要从各保存位置自行删除。游戏没有可注销账号。支持邮件按处理请求和履行适用义务的需要保留，不承诺固定期限；可联系隐私邮箱请求删除支持信息，但可能受适用义务限制。

儿童与安全

游戏不会要求玩家提供年龄或个人资料，运营者不会通过游戏玩法有意索取儿童个人信息。儿童需要支持时应由家长或监护人联系。如果你认为儿童个人信息已发送给我们，请联系隐私邮箱，以便核查处理。任何安全措施都不能保证绝对安全。

内部版本及更新

内部开发版本可在本地保存调参预设、测试历史，并允许测试者主动导入导出JSON；公开Release游戏不提供这些开发控制入口。数据实践变化时，我们会更新政策，并在App与网站显示一致的版本和生效日期；未来功能不会被描述成当前已存在的实践。
